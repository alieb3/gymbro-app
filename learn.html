Training

Stop 1 to 3 reps short of failure

1–3reps left in the tank

The last reps before failure do the most work. Going all the way adds fatigue without a clear extra gain.

Currier 2026 · Refalo 2023 · a debate
Training

Rest until the next set will be good

2–3minutes on big lifts, 1–2 on small ones

Rest long enough to do the next set well. Resting under a minute may cost a little growth and strength.

Nutrition

3 tips

Enough protein, spread over the day. Supplements are a footnote.

Nutrition

Eat enough protein every day

1.4–2.0g of protein per kg of body weight, a day

If you train, eat about this much protein, spread over your meals. For most people the gain levels off near 1.6.

Jäger 2017 · Morton 2018
Nutrition

Spread protein over 3 or 4 meals

3–4meals with about 25 to 40 g of protein each

Several solid servings give several rises in muscle building. Your daily total still matters more than the timing.

Nutrition

Creatine is the one supplement worth knowing

3–5g of creatine monohydrate a day

The best-studied supplement for strength and muscle, and safe for healthy adults at this amount. With kidney disease, ask your doctor first.

Kreider 2017

Rest and recovery

3 tips

Muscle is built between sessions.

Rest and recovery

Sleep at least 7 hours

7hours of sleep or more, each night

Short sleep makes training harder and slowed muscle building in lab studies. After a bad night, train anyway.

Rest and recovery

Soreness is not the score

Soreness after a new or harder workout is normal and fades in a few days. Sharp pain, pain in a joint or swelling is different: ease off.

Cheung 2003 · Schoenfeld 2013
Rest and recovery

Take an easy week when you feel run down

Planned easy weeks are optional. A lighter week, or even a week off, cost little or no muscle in trials.

Walking and running

Move 150 minutes a week

150minutes of brisk walking a week, or 75 of running

Regular aerobic work lowers the risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, several cancers and depression. More brings more.

Bull 2020

Gym basics

6

Short answers for your first weeks in a gym.

How do I pick a weight?

Choose a weight you can lift for the reps shown with good form and about two or three reps still left. Too easy? Add a little next set. Gymbro remembers it for next time.

What does “reps in reserve” mean?

How many more reps you could have done. Stopping 1 to 3 short of failure builds strength and muscle almost as well as failing, with less soreness and risk.

Do I need to warm up?

Five minutes of easy cardio and a couple of light sets of your first exercise is enough. For heavy lifts, open the exercise menu and add warm-up sets.

How do I set up a machine?

Line the pivot (the joint of the machine) up with the joint you are moving, set the seat so the handles start at mid-chest or the pad sits on the right spot, and start light.

Gym etiquette

Wipe the bench after you, put plates and dumbbells back, and let people work in between your sets on a machine. Ask “how many sets left?” if you are waiting.

Sore the next day?

Normal after a new exercise, and it fades within a few days. Sharp or joint pain is different: stop that exercise and swap it for another.

Heard at the gym

10

Straight answers to what gets said, each with its evidence. Share one with a friend or your coach.

Will lifting make me bulky?

No. Muscle grows slowly; a bulky look takes years of training and eating for it.

Will sit-ups burn my belly fat?

No. Fat comes off the whole body; ab work builds the muscle underneath.

Should I skip training on my period?

No need. Go by how you feel that day, and take a lighter day when you need one.
