Back of thigh

Semimembranosus

Latin (TA2)
Musculus semimembranosus TA2 2642
Academic Persian
ماهیچه نیم‌پرده‌ای
Gym name
Inner hamstring, deep
Back of thighCrosses two jointsPennateHip hingeIsolationHamstrings

What it does

Bends the knee and drives the hip back; the biggest hamstring by cross-section.

Starts at
Sitting bone (ischial tuberosity)
Attaches to
Back of the inner tibial condyle
Joints
Hip · Knee
Pennation15.1°
Fibre length6.9cm
Cross-section (PCSA)18.4cm²

Exercises for this group

  • Seated leg curl3 × 8–15 · Machine

Rendered by Gymbro from the Z-Anatomy 3D atlas, which is built on BodyParts3D. Both views are orthographic, lit from the upper left. Names follow Terminologia Anatomica (TA2).

Pennate muscles pack fibres at an angle to the tendon. More fibres fit in the same space, but each pulls along the tendon with only cos θ of its force. Angles are cadaver means from Ward et al. 2009.
